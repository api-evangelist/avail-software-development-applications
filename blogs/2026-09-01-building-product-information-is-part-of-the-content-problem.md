AVAIL Founder and CEO Randall Stevens has been working to strengthen the connection between design firms and building product manufacturers (BPMs) since before AVAIL’s product launch in 2017. That work led to the AVAIL Select Channels in 2021, making trusted manufacturer Revit content more accessible to AVAIL users, and continued with AVAIL’s acquisition of gridBPM in 2026. Below, Randall explores the AEC industry’s content problem, the role building product information plays in it, and how AVAIL is working to create a better path forward.

# Avail Project

> Avail is verifiable data availability (DA) and cross-chain coordination infrastructure for Web3. It helps developers build apps and rollups that reach users, liquidity, and assets across every major blockchain from day one, without worrying which chain an asset or app lives on.

## Core Products

- [Avail DA](https://www.availproject.org/da.html): Cryptographically verifiable, horizontally scaling data availability layer for high-throughput rollups, validiums, and L2/L3 chains. ~20s finality, KZG commitments, DAS light clients.

- [Avail Nexus](https://www.availproject.org/nexus.html): Cross-chain coordination layer that gives apps unified balances, bridgeless flows, and access to users and liquidity across 10+ major blockchains.

- [Avail Deposits](https://www.availproject.org/deposits.html): One-click deposit infrastructure for dApps. Handles bridging, gas, and swaps automatically. Integrate with 1 line of code. Audited. 99.9% success rate.

## Integrations

- [Avail Widgets](https://docs.availproject.org/): Pre-built UI components for cross-chain deposits and coordination.
- [Nexus SDK](https://docs.availproject.org/nexus/nexus-quickstart): Build multi-chain apps with a single integration.
- [FastBridge](https://fastbridge.availproject.org/): Cross-chain bridge for USDC, USDT, ETH and other tokens across EVM chains, powered by Avail Nexus.

## Avail Deposits for Prediction Markets

> Avail Deposits is embedded cross-chain deposit infrastructure that helps prediction markets make it easier for users to fund positions. Users can deposit with eligible assets held across supported chains without leaving the app to manually bridge, swap, switch networks, or acquire gas.

This page is intended for teams building prediction markets, outcome markets, forecasting platforms, and other applications where users need to deposit a specific collateral asset on a specific chain before participating.

### The problem

Prediction markets may require one collateral asset on one destination chain, while a user's funds can be spread across multiple chains and tokens. Sending users to external bridges, swaps, and gas-acquisition steps interrupts the journey between discovering a market and funding a position. Every handoff creates another opportunity for the deposit to be delayed, reduced, or abandoned.

### How Avail Deposits works

Avail Deposits presents eligible balances across supported chains in a unified view. The user chooses how much to spend or the exact amount the prediction market should receive. The embedded flow handles the required routing and gas, then completes the deposit inside the prediction market experience.

The typical journey is:

1. Select a market or outcome and set a position amount.
2. View eligible balances across supported chains.
3. Choose an exact-in or exact-out deposit.
4. Let the flow handle routing and required gas.
5. Receive the required collateral in the destination app and continue to the position.

### Benefits for prediction markets

- Keep high-intent users inside the application's funding journey.
- Allow more eligible user capital across supported chains and assets to contribute to a deposit.
- Reduce the time between discovering a market and funding a position.
- Reduce deposit-related support questions about networks, gas, required assets, and funding amounts.

### Core capabilities

- **Unified multichain balance spending:** Eligible balances across supported chains and assets can contribute to one deposit.
- **Exact-in:** The user enters the amount they want to spend, and the flow calculates the destination output.
- **Exact-out:** The user enters the exact amount the prediction market must receive, and the flow calculates the required input across eligible balances.
- **Gas handling:** Gas required to complete the transaction is handled within the flow.
- **Embedded UI:** The deposit journey remains within the prediction market experience.
- **Configurable integration:** Teams can choose the destination chain and asset, define supported source coverage, and adapt the interface to their design system.

Avail Deposits may also surface supported fiat on-ramp, off-ramp, and QR-code deposit routes. Availability depends on the relevant provider, asset, region, and current product coverage.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How can a prediction market accept deposits from multiple chains?

Add an embedded cross-chain deposit flow that accepts eligible assets from supported source chains and delivers the required collateral to the destination application. Routing, bridging, and swaps are handled within the funding journey.

#### What is the difference between exact-in and exact-out?

Exact-in starts with the amount the user wants to spend and calculates the destination output. Exact-out starts with the exact amount the prediction market needs to receive, then calculates the required input using eligible balances and available routes.

#### Do users need to bridge manually?

No. The user remains inside the application while the required cross-chain routing is handled within the deposit flow.

#### Do users need native gas tokens on every chain?

No. Gas required to complete the transaction is handled within the flow, so users do not need to separately acquire native gas tokens for each step.

#### Can the widget match the prediction market's interface?

Yes. Teams can configure supported chains and tokens and adapt the widget interface to fit their application experience.

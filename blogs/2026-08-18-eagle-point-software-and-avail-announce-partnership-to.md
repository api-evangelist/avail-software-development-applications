LEXINGTON, Kentucky and DUBUQUE, Iowa, August 18, 2026 - Software company AVAIL – which creates content management system (CMS) solutions for the global architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) industry – and Eagle Point Software – creator of Pinnacle Series – today announced a new partnership. Through the partnership, AVAIL training content will be added to Pinnacle Series, expanding the platform’s growing library of software training and practical learning resources for architecture, engineering, and construction professionals. AVAIL is AEC’s all-in-one content management solution.

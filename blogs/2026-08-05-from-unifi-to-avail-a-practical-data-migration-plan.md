The content management landscape for the architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) industry is changing. If your firm is still relying on UNIFI for Revit content management, the clock is ticking to transfer your firm’s data to a new content management system (CMS). The biggest risk isn’t losing the tool, it’s losing access to the content and workflows your team depends on.

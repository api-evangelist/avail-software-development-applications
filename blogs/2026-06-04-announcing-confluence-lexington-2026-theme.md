Hosted by software company AVAIL , Confluence Lexington 2026 is an invite-only professional development event for architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) technology leaders that will take place October 8-10, 2026. The program theme this year is The Future-Ready AEC Firm. Over three days, leaders will explore how firms can adapt to evolving technology, workforce expectations, and business demands by building stronger foundations in people, process, data, and project knowledge.
